About Us

20+ Years of Experience in TV Service

VANDANA ELECTRONICS is an LED TV service centre based in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, with 20+ years of experience in TV servicing.

We specialise in servicing LED TVs from different companies and brands. Our services include LED TV repair, technical servicing, display-related repairs and LED TV display replacement , subject to availability.
